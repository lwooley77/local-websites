## Don't say on the call
- Prices, hours, or "we take texts": all unconfirmed (see Missing in each STATUS.md). Ask them instead.
- That the photos or review quotes are approved. They come from public pages, and the owner must approve them first.

## One-line opener
"Hi, is this the owner? I'm local, and I noticed [hook]. I built a free preview. Can I text you the link?"
