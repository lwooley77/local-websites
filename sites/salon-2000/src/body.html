About the salon

Same plaza, same owner, since 1999

Rhonda Clark opened Salon 2000 in 1999 in Hacienda Plaza, on the corner of Sparks Blvd and E Prater Way. She still does hair here, and the salon has grown into a full team for hair, nails, skin, makeup and Botox.
