Quilas Cosmo Corner

A full-service cosmetology studio in Carson City: nails, hair, color, skin, waxing and tinting, all in one place.

Open the live siteGet one for your business

The goal

Give a new studio one clear place that shows everything it offers, with the prices in plain view, and makes it easy to call, message or book.

What I built

  • Every service and price, organized by category
  • A gallery of her real nail work
  • An online booking calendar
  • Call, message and directions, one tap each
  • Hours and location, with an "open now" note
  • An FAQ that answers the common questions

A note

This is the site I built for my wife's business. It's the first one, and it's where I learned what customers need to see in the first few seconds.
