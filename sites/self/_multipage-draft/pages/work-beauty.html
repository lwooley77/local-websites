A retro family salon.

A concept for a long-running neighborhood salon with a warm, friendly feel.

The idea

A neighborhood salon doesn't need a cold, glossy site. This concept leans into a retro salon look: a cherry-red chair, a hood dryer, mint and a checkerboard border. It feels like the place, and it works the moment a customer opens it.

What it does

  • A Call button on every screen
  • Services for men and women in one place
  • Hours that are easy to find
  • The real star rating, linked to the source
This is a concept. I built it from the salon's public listings. It isn't a live site and the salon hasn't hired me. If you run it and want it removed, email me.
