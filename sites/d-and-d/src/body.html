About the shop

A cut before work

D & D Barbers is a walk-in barbershop at 3024 Kietzke Lane in Reno. The doors open at 7 AM, so you can get a haircut on the way to the job and still clock in on time.

The barbers do clipper cuts, fades, line-ups, beard trims, head shaves and hot towel shaves, on every type of hair. No app, no account. Pull into the lot, come in and take a seat.
