About the salon

A Midtown salon on Vassar Street

Slice is a hair salon at 137 Vassar Street, in the heart of Reno's Midtown Arts District.

The work covers the full range: cuts for women, men and kids, color from soft balayage to bright pinks and teals, keratin and permanent straightening, hand-tied extensions, plus brows and waxing.

Reviewers keep coming back to the same things: a clean, bright salon, a relaxed feel, and stylists who listen and take their time.
