The Jack of All Fades

That's D'Raun Manning. He's a Reno guy: Hug High class of 2008, basketball coach at Wooster High, and the owner of this shop on Smithridge Drive, where he works with a team of barbers and cosmetologists.

Nevada men's basketball doesn't have an official barber, but a lot of the Wolf Pack sits in D'Raun's chair. Players have described the shop as a place to hang out and a support system, not just a haircut.

The idea behind it all: build a stronger community, one cut at a time. The shop has even opened its doors for a free haircut day.

"I hope that when they leave, they want to come back."
