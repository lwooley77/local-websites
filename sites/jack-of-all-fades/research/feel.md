# Feel: Jack Of All Fades

## Feel words
1. **Card-sharp** - the name is a playing-card pun, and this is Reno. The Jack court card is the obvious, ownable symbol: mirrored portrait, corner indices, card-back pattern.
2. **Hometown** - D'Raun is a Hug High '08 grad who coaches at Wooster. Reno-born, Reno-raised. The Arch, the Truckee, the hills.
3. **Locker-room warm** - Wolf Pack players hang out here; the shop is a gathering spot and a support system, not a spa.
4. **Sharp lines** - fades and designs are about precise graduated lines. Stacked lines that get thinner = the fade motif.
5. **Open door** - walk-ins welcome, free haircut days, "one cut at a time."

## Palette (ivory card stock + black and red ink)

## Type
- Display: Anton (tall condensed sign-painter weight, reads like barbershop window lettering)
- Card indices + big numerals: DM Serif Display (playing-card serif "J")
- Labels: IBM Plex Mono 500 (ticket / card tag)
- Body: Karla 400/500/700

## Motifs
1. Mirrored Jack court card, with a fade haircut drawn as stacked lines thinning upward
2. Corner indices "J" + custom pip (a straight razor in place of a suit)
3. Card-back lattice pattern (thin diamond lattice) for dark panels
4. Four suits re-cut as tools: spade = clipper guard, heart, diamond, club as section bullets
5. The fade gradient: 7 horizontal rules getting thinner (used as section dividers)
6. Barber pole stripe in red/ink as a thin rule
7. Reno Arch silhouette (no lettering) in the footer
8. A fanned hand of cards for the services strip ends
9. Basketball seam arc tucked into the About card (Wolf Pack connection)

## Ambient touches
- The hero Jack card tilts a few degrees with scroll (set straight from scrollY, off under reduced motion)
- A thin barber-pole stripe in the open badge drifts slowly
- Footer night scene: a few window lights in the hills blink in once on reveal
