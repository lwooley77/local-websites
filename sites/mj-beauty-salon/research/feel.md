# Feel: MJ Beauty Salon

## Feel words
- **Neighborly**: family-owned, mother-daughter team, regulars who have come back for 25 years.
- **Old-school**: 40+ years on the same street; a classic hometown beauty shop, not a trendy studio.
- **Quick and unfussy**: known for cuts done in under 20 minutes and fair prices.
- **Warm**: reviews keep saying friendly, "the best", "family owned".
- **Airport Road**: the shop sits on Airport Rd near Carson City's little airport, under the Sierra foothills.
