A barber shop on Highway 395.

A concept for a small-town barber shop that sits right on the highway through town.

The idea

Most barber sites look alike. This one takes its look from the road the shop is on: a green street-sign logo, a highway shield and a price list laid out like a road sign. A customer who sees it knows exactly whose shop it is.

What it does

  • An "open now" note that follows the shop's hours
  • Call, text and directions, one tap each
  • Services and prices in one clear list
  • Real customer words, only quoted exactly
  • An FAQ for walk-ins, hours and payment
This is a concept. I built it from the shop's public listings. It isn't a live site and the shop hasn't hired me. If you run it and want it removed, email me.
