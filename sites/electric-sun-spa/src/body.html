About Electric Sun

A neighborhood retreat on Main Street

A spa “home away from home,” where you can drop in, be yourself, and leave feeling fabulous.

Electric Sun opened in Fernley in 2003 and grew into a full-service salon and day spa in 2007. Today one visit can cover hair and color, nails and pedicures, lash extensions and brows, facials and HydraFacial, waxing, massage, spray tans and teeth whitening.

Owner Shannon Ceresola and her team of stylists, nail techs, estheticians and massage therapists keep the prices reasonable and the hours easy to work around, with late appointments Tuesday through Thursday.

  • Walk-ins accepted
  • Free parking
  • Open until 7 PM Tue to Thu
  • Kid friendly
  • WiFi, beer and wine
  • Accessible entrance

The boutique carries Pureology, Nicolas, Designer Skin tanning products and Lira Clinical skin care, plus clothing, handbags, jewelry and gifts.

Bridal party, birthday or girls' day? Electric Sun can set up a custom day for your group. Call to plan it.
