2About the shop

Dan and Jeff, on Main Street.

Main Street Barber Shop is where Dan and Jeff cut hair, on Highway 395, which is Main Street through Gardnerville.

They do haircuts for grown-ups and kids, fades and buzz cuts, hot towel shaves, head shaves, and beard and moustache trims.

Walk in when a chair is open, or call ahead and ask about the wait. There's street parking by the shop.
