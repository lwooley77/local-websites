#02 / The shop

About the shop

Cutz Unlimited is a barbershop at 4938 S Virginia St in Reno, run by owner and stylist Christian Shaeffer, who specializes in men's haircuts.

The chairs here handle fades, skin fades, tapers and scissor cuts, along with beard trims, head shaves and hot towel shaves. Kids are welcome, and so are women's cuts.

Reviewers keep coming back to the same things: stylists who take their time, listen to what you want, and remember how you wear it. One regular has been seeing the same stylist here for 13 years.
