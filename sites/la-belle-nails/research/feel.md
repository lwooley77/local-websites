# Feel
- Parisian: "La Belle" is French; lean into a small Paris salon front (awning, arched window, script-free serif).
- Calm neighborhood: Sharlands Ave in Northwest Reno (89523), a residential shopping strip near the foothills.
- Clean and polished: services are classic manicure, gel, pedicure, nail art. French tip is the natural signature shape.
- Fresh, not loud: clearly unlike LV Nails (red/burgundy, polish bottle).
