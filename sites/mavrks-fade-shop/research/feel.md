# Feel
- Sharp: a fade shop, the whole menu is cuts, beards, brows and freestyle designs.
- Independent: the name is MAVRKS (mavericks), five barbers each with their own book.
- Late-hour: early bird and after-hours slots are a real offer on every senior barber's page.
- Straight-talking: cash only, 2-hour notice, $25 no-show. Rules written plainly.

## Palette
--ink:#0f0f10; --ink-2:#18181a; --line:#2c2c30; --bone:#efe8dc; --mute:#b9b2a6; --red:#ff5a47 (5.9:1 on ink); --red-deep:#c8321f; --steel:#8fa3ad
## Fonts
Anton (display, condensed poster caps), IBM Plex Mono 500 (labels, like clipper guard stamps), Work Sans 400/600 (body).
## Motifs
fade gradient built from hatch lines; clipper guard numbers 0 / 1 / 2 / 3 as section markers; barber pole diagonal stripe; straight razor line; booth/chair numbers per barber; price-board rows in mono; "Bldg I" plate; cash-only stamp.
## Ambient
hatch fade lines drift a few px with scrollY; pole stripe slides once on load; nothing loops.
