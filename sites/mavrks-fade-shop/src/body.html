02 / The shop

Five chairs. One shop.

MAVRKS is a fade shop in Building I at 1801 E William St, run by Bill, the shop owner. Beto, Bryan, Martin and Juan work alongside him, and every barber books their own clients.

Every barber does haircuts and beards. Freestyle designs, brow clean-ups, perms and color depend on who you book: perms are done by Bill, Bryan and Juan, and Bryan also does color and highlights.

Can't make it in during the day? Bill, Beto and Bryan take early bird or after-hours appointments. Text your barber to set one up.
