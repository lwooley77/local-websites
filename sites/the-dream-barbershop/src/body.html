The Shop

Six barbers on California Ave

The Dream is Rodrigo Gomez's shop, at %%STREET%% in Reno. Six barbers work the floor, and each one keeps a book on Squire, so you can choose who cuts your hair.

Inside: gold-trimmed chairs under a lit hex ceiling, skate decks on the wall and the shop's monogram set into the floor.

The menu covers haircuts, beard work, eyebrows, designs and shaves for grown-ups, and kids cuts from age 2. Walk in and take the next open chair, or book ahead and skip the wait.

Follow the shop's latest cuts on Instagram at @thedreambarbershopreno.
