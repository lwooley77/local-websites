A neighborhood salon that feels like family

Jessie’s Salon is a locally owned family salon serving Northwest Reno and the surrounding area with haircuts, color, waxing, perms, styling and more. Owner Jessie and her team work out of Canyon Center on N McCarran, seven days a week.

Clients keep coming back for the same reasons: stylists who listen and check the length with you before they cut, a lively, down-to-earth vibe, and prices that stay fair. Bring the kids and bring your partner. Walk in, or book ahead.

Frequently Asked Questions

Anything else? Call us at (775) 737-9303.

Do you take walk-ins?
Yes, walk-ins are welcome. If you’d like a set time with a specific stylist, book online or call (775) 737-9303.
How much is a haircut?
Our online menu lists women’s and men’s cuts from $30 and kids’ cuts from $25. Color, texture and add-ons are priced by service. See the menu above or call for a quote.
Who do you serve?
Everyone: women, men and kids. Plenty of our clients bring the whole family.
Do you offer anything besides hair?
Yes. We do brow, face and body waxing and brow tinting, and we also offer nails, lashes and permanent makeup. Call to check availability.
Where do I park?
Free parking right out front in Canyon Center, 10580 N McCarran Blvd #106. Our entrance and restroom are ADA-accessible with no steps.
What’s your cancellation policy?
Please give at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule; late cancellations may be charged a $75 fee. We accept Visa, Mastercard, Discover, debit and cash.
