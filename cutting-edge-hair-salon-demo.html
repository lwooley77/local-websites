About

A neighborhood salon, not a chain

Cutting Edge opened on South Carson Street in 2015 and has been cutting Carson City hair ever since. The idea is simple: skilled stylists, personal service, and a cut you do not have to think about until your next visit.

Come in for a quick trim between appointments, a fresh color, or a full restyle before a big day. Walk-ins are welcome, and regulars can book their favorite stylist.
