## How to work this list
1. **Walk in, don't cold-DM.** Start with #1 and #11, which are in the same building, then #3 (he lists his email). Show the demo on your phone. Ask: "Can I send you the link?"
2. **Have a demo ready first.** A 60-second look at their own name, prices and photos on a real site closes deals. A description doesn't.
3. **One simple offer.** For example: $X setup, then $Y/month for hosting and updates. Pick numbers you'd be comfortable defending. Don't make the site free. Free work gets ignored.
4. **Get a yes on photos** before anything goes public. Use their Instagram photos for the private demo only.
5. Track status here: `new → visited → demo sent → yes/no`.
